# Brief: high-fidelity, real-scale three.js models for the 3D Composite Skill Lab

You are replacing low-poly placeholder models of school-lab equipment with high-fidelity three.js models.
Accuracy of real-world dimensions and resemblance to the catalogue photo are the two things that matter.
`js/hifi/agri-instruments.js` (batch 1) is the quality bar: study it before you start, and match or beat it.

## Where things are (working directory is `lab3d/`)
- `js/models.js`: the current placeholder builders, `ITEM_MODELS[sno]`. Read your items' placeholders for origin and pose conventions. Do NOT edit this file.
- `js/kit.js`: helpers you may import from `'../kit.js'`: `M(color,{r,m,o,e,ei,side,map})` cached MeshStandardMaterial (r=roughness, m=metalness, o=opacity), `B(w,h,d,mat)` box with bottom at y=0, `C(rTop,rBot,h,mat,seg)` cylinder bottom at y=0, `S(r,mat)`, `Tor(r,t,mat,arc)`, `Rod(a,b,r,mat)`, `G(...children)`, `at(obj,x,y,z,ry,rx,rz)`, `tex(w,h,draw)` canvas texture, `Panel(w,h,px,draw)` textured plane facing +z, `STEEL() ALU() GLASS() BLACK() WOOD()`, `FONT`. Do NOT edit kit.js.
- `catalogue-data.js`: the item list (sno, item, spec, qty, zone, img). `spec` often contains real sizes.
- `../catalogue/<img>`: the catalogue photo for each item (webp). Look at the photo of every item you model. It is the visual truth for shape, colour, proportions and details.
- three.js r170 via `import * as THREE from 'three'`; addons via `three/addons/...` (`RoundedBoxGeometry`, `mergeGeometries`, etc.).
- `review.html?file=js/hifi/<your file>.js` shows photo / placeholder / your model side by side with measured sizes (the user's local server serves it at http://localhost:8080/lab3d/).

## Contract for every builder (the room breaks if any of these fail)
- `export const HIFI = { [sno]: () => THREE.Group, ... }` from your output file. Builders take no arguments and must not throw.
- Units are metres. Origin at bottom-centre of the item's resting footprint (lowest point at y=0, centred in x and z), front facing +z.
- Keep the placeholder's **resting pose**: hand tools that hang on the tool rail (hoe, trowel, khurpi, sickle, rake, spade) stand upright, handle down. Small tools on a bench lie flat. Containers stand upright.
- Wall-mounted items (first-aid box 94, stature meter 66, pegboard 88, router 91, apron rail 98): read the placeholder and keep its origin exactly, so the item sits flush on the wall where it is placed now.
- Set `castShadow = true` and `receiveShadow = true` on meshes (tiny parts < 2 cm may skip castShadow).
- No external files, no network, no textures from disk: draw labels, LCDs and printing with canvas via `tex`/`Panel`.
- Triangle budget per item: ≤ 3,000 (≤ 8,000 for appliances, stove, mixer, oven, NFT rack, cupboard, worktable, BP machine, CPR mannequin). Share materials and geometries between duplicates.
- Plain JS ES module, only the imports above. No brand names or logos; neutral text like "ISI", "2 kg", "5 L" is fine.

## Method (per item)
1. Decide real dimensions: numbers in `spec` win ("2x1 ft, height 4in", "Diameter 14 inch", "5 Ltr", "Blade 18in", "200mm", "6×4 ft"); otherwise the typical size of that exact product sold in India (ISI single-burner stove ≈ 30×10×30 cm; 800 W mixer base ≈ 22×20×18 cm with 1 L jar; OTG 25 L ≈ 48×30×36 cm; steel lockable cupboard 91×198×48 cm; first-aid box ≈ 36×28×12 cm; 15 L bucket Ø32×30 cm). Volume specs must agree with the geometry. Final size within ±5 %.
2. Silhouette: `LatheGeometry` for anything round, `ExtrudeGeometry` from a `THREE.Shape` (with a small bevel) for flat profiles, `TubeGeometry` for handles, spouts, hoses, cables and wire, `RoundedBoxGeometry` for appliances and boxes.
3. Details that make it recognisable at 1–2 m: rims, lips, lids, knobs, hinges, spouts, nozzles, rivets, ribbed grips, dial ticks, LCD readouts, canvas-printed labels.
4. Materials matched to the photo: brushed steel (m≈0.7, r≈0.3; keep metalness ≤ 0.75 so it doesn't render black), aluminium, glossy HDPE plastic (r≈0.4), clear PP/glass (o≈0.35), wood (warm brown, r≈0.7), rubber (r≈0.9).
