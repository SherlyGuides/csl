# Raw walkthrough video prompt (Gemini / Veo), no labels, loops

Use Veo image-to-video, one clip per shot. Use a screenshot of that corner from the 3D lab as each clip's start frame.
Shot 9 uses the Shot 1 start image as its last frame, so the joined video loops.

## Style block (top of every shot)
Photoreal architectural interior walkthrough of a bright, modern school "Composite Skill Lab", about 36 × 32 ft. Cream walls with an orange accent band at waist height, a grey speckled floor, and soft daylight from three tall windows on the right wall plus even ceiling lights. Shot on a smooth gimbal/drone with slow, constant-speed movement: no shake, no cuts inside the clip, no zoom jumps. Use 16:9, 24 fps, natural colours and sharp focus. RAW FOOTAGE: absolutely no text, titles, captions, labels, logos added in post, UI, watermarks or graphics. Keep only what physically exists in the room. Ambient room tone only: no music, no voiceover. The room is tidy and realistic, with nothing floating or warped and real-world scale throughout.

## Shots (8 s each)
1. **Bird's eye opening:** the camera starts high above the lab in a top-down view, as if the ceiling were removed. The whole room is visible: five wooden worktables with navy chairs in the centre (40 seats), a black-and-white robot arena mat between them, and equipment zones along every wall. The camera slowly descends and tilts forward into an angled overview toward the entrance doors.
2. **Entrance and teaching wall:** from the double entrance doors, the camera glides down the centre aisle toward the front wall: a large interactive display flanked by two whiteboards, with the school crest logo painted above, and storage cupboards along the wall.
3. **Robotics build floor:** a low, slow glide across the worktables, with robotics kits spread out (gears, motors, circuit boards, a small assembled robot). The camera passes over the robot arena mat, where a small line-following robot tracks the black line.
4. **Build bench and kit almirah:** a lateral track along the left wall: a work bench with a robot build and a blue kit tub, a pegboard tool wall with neatly hung pliers, screwdrivers and spanners, then a tall steel almirah with stacked kit tubs.
5. **AI computer wall:** a counter with three desktop PCs whose monitors show code and an AI chat screen. Webcams and headphones hang on the wall, with yellow stools in front.
6. **Food production kitchen:** a slow pan along the back-left kitchen: a white fridge, a long counter with a steel sink, two gas stoves with cookware, a mixer and two ovens. Steel sieves, ladles and utensils hang on the wall, and two wall shelves hold jars and containers.
7. **Safety corner and healthcare bay:** a red fire extinguisher, a sand bucket stand, a first-aid box, and a PPE rack of aprons. Then a lilac medical shelf with a BP monitor, stethoscope, thermometer and pulse oximeter, a small vitals table and a CPR mannequin.
8. **Agriculture wall:** a track along the sunlit right wall: a white hydroponic NFT rack with green lettuce, a counter with soil-testing kits and seed trays, garden tools hung on a wooden rail under the windows, potted plants, and long tools in a corner stand.
9. **Return to bird's eye (loop):** the camera rises from the centre aisle, pulls back and tilts down to the same top-down view as the very first frame of Shot 1. It decelerates smoothly and ends on exactly that composition.
